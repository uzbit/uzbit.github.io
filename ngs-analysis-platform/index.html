Everything after the sequencer, in one place

Upload your sequencing files, choose an analysis, and download your results. There's nothing to install and no command line — it all runs in your web browser. Read cleanup, sample sorting, diversity, RNA-seq, and assembly are all built in, and each analysis can start from the results of the last one, so you can run a whole workflow end to end. Large studies run on powerful cloud computers, so a big dataset finishes just as smoothly as a small one. Works with Oxford Nanopore, Illumina, and PacBio data.

Analyses built in

Demultiplexing

Sort a pooled sequencing run back into its individual samples by their barcodes. Error-tolerant matching copes with noisy reads and double-barcoded Nanopore kits, weak reads are filtered out, and QC plots show how cleanly each sample came apart.

Clustering

Group near-identical sequences together and keep one representative for each group. Use it to trim a reference set down to its unique strains, or to condense repetitive reads before analysis — without losing track of how abundant each one was.

Diversity Analysis

Match your reads against a reference set to see what's in each sample and how much of it, then get the standard diversity measures — richness, Shannon, Simpson, and evenness — plus abundance tables ready to drop into a figure.

Differential Expression

Measure gene expression from RNA-seq and find the genes that go up or down between two conditions. You get volcano plots, PCA, and expression heatmaps alongside the full results table — computed with the widely used DESeq2 method.

Gene Set Enrichment

See which biological pathways are driving your results. Gene set enrichment (GSEA) takes your ranked list of genes and highlights the pathways and gene sets that stand out, with clear plots of the strongest hits.

De novo Assembly

Rebuild full-length sequences from your reads when you have no reference to compare against. You get the assembled sequences and standard quality stats like N50, plus a dedicated mode for mixed amplicon libraries such as antibody repertoires.

Built to handle real sequencing runs

Fast, even on big data

The platform is tuned to move quickly through large datasets. A smart first pass skips the comparisons that can't matter and spends its time only on the ones that do — so analyses that used to take hours come back much sooner, with no shortcuts on accuracy.

Scales when you need it

Your analyses run on powerful cloud computers that switch on only when you have work to do. A large study gets the horsepower it needs, and you're not paying for a machine that sits idle between experiments.

Nothing to install

Everything happens in your browser — upload your files, watch each analysis run in real time, and download the results when it's done. Keep your work organized in projects, and pin the reference files you reach for again and again.

Three steps from data to answer

1. Upload

Add your sequencing files, along with any reference or barcode files, and group them together in a project.

2. Choose an analysis

Pick an analysis and select your files — or the results of an earlier step. Every setting starts from a sensible default, so you can simply run it.

3. Download results

Watch the numbers and plots come together as it runs, then download everything — ready to share or publish — when it's finished.

Results you can publish

Every analysis hands you a complete set of results to download: the numbers, plain-language summaries, and ready-made figures — diversity tables, volcano plots and heatmaps, QC reports, assembly stats, and more. The exact settings you used are saved with every run, so your work is always reproducible.

Who it's for

Molecular biology and synthetic biology labs that want answers from their sequencing data without becoming bioinformaticians: amplicon and 16S community profiling, Nanopore QC, antibody and variant-library characterization, directed-evolution screens, and RNA-seq.
